# Commander Space

**Commander Space** is the canonical name for Sigma's experience / decision / product layer, through which relevant understanding becomes human **awareness, decision, and action**. ("Commander Experience" in some newer documents refers to this same layer — see `../../GLOSSARY.md`.)

## What this product owns
- The commander-facing experience where trusted understanding is presented for decision.
- Attention, prioritization, and decision/action UX built on top of shared capabilities.
- Its spaces: **Headquarters Workspace** and **My Space**.

## What this product does NOT own
- domain truth / operational state → **Operations**
- spatial computation → **Geography**
- meaning synthesis → **Context & Meaning**
- final operational decision authority — **decisions remain with humans** (human-in-the-loop)

## Boundaries with neighboring areas
- Commander Space **consumes/applies** shared capabilities, including **Actionable Experience** (`../../04-SHARED-CAPABILITIES/Actionable-Experience/`), rather than defining them.
- The earlier "Operational Repositories" product concept (PR-017) has been moved to `../../99-ARCHIVE/Historical/Product-Concepts/` and is **no longer a current child product** of Commander Space.
