# Sigma Discovery

## Purpose

The Sigma Discovery documents the observations, insights, assumptions, and evidence that led to the creation of Sigma.

Its purpose is to establish the rationale behind Sigma's strategic direction and provide traceability between operational challenges and the principles defined throughout the Sigma Knowledge Base.

Unlike the Sigma Doctrine, which defines what Sigma is, this document explains why Sigma became necessary.

---

## Audience

This document is intended for leadership, product teams, architects, researchers, and stakeholders seeking to understand the reasoning behind Sigma's operating model and strategic direction.

---

## Scope

This document captures:

- The operational problem space.
- Key discovery findings.
- Observed organizational patterns.
- Design implications.
- Strategic assumptions.
- Evidence supporting Sigma's core concepts.
- Outstanding questions requiring future validation.

---

## Out of Scope

This document does not define:

- Sigma's operating model.
- Platform architecture.
- Product specifications.
- Technical solutions.
- Delivery plans.

Those subjects are documented in their respective knowledge domains.
