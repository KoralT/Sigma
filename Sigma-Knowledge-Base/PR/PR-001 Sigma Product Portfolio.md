# Sigma Product Portfolio

## Purpose

The Sigma Product Portfolio defines the operational products and user-facing capabilities delivered by Sigma.

Building upon the Sigma Operating Model and Platform Architecture, this document describes how Sigma's conceptual capabilities are packaged into coherent products that support operational users.

The purpose of this document is not to define product specifications, but to establish the product landscape of Sigma.

---

## Audience

This document is intended for product leadership, operational leadership, architecture teams, engineering leadership, UX teams, and delivery teams responsible for planning and evolving Sigma's operational products.

---

## Scope

This document defines:

- Sigma's product landscape.
- The responsibility of each product.
- The relationship between products.
- Product ownership boundaries.
- Shared capabilities across products.

---

## Out of Scope

This document intentionally excludes:

- User stories.
- Product requirements.
- UX flows.
- UI designs.
- Technical implementation.
- Release planning.
- Delivery plans.

Detailed product specifications are documented in dedicated product documents.
