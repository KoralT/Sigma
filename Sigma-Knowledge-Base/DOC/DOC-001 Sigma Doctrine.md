# Sigma Doctrine

## Purpose

The Sigma Doctrine is the foundational document of Sigma.

It establishes the enduring philosophy that guides the organization's evolution and defines the principles upon which every operational capability, product, and architectural decision is built.

Rather than describing solutions, systems, or technologies, this document defines the beliefs, assumptions, and transformation model that shape Sigma as an organization. These principles are intended to remain stable even as products, capabilities, and technologies evolve.

---

## Mission

Sigma exists to enable organizations to make better operational decisions by establishing a shared foundation for understanding operational reality.

Its mission is not to build isolated systems, but to establish the organizational foundations that allow operational information to become shared understanding, trusted context, and ultimately better decision-making.

---

## Audience

The Sigma Doctrine is intended for leaders responsible for shaping Sigma's direction and execution.

This includes executive leadership, operational leadership, product leadership, architecture leadership, and engineering leadership who require a shared understanding of Sigma's long-term vision and operating philosophy.

---

## Scope

This document defines the conceptual foundations of Sigma.

It explains why transformation is required, articulates Sigma's strategic thesis, introduces its operating philosophy and conceptual operating model, and establishes the principles that guide every future capability developed by the organization.

The document intentionally focuses on enduring concepts rather than implementation.

---

## Out of Scope

The Sigma Doctrine intentionally excludes implementation details.

It does not describe products, user experiences, features, technical architecture, engineering decisions, delivery plans, or implementation roadmaps.

These topics are documented independently to ensure that the doctrine remains stable while the organization's capabilities continue to evolve.
