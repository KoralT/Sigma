# Sigma Operating Model

## Purpose

The Sigma Operating Model defines how Sigma transforms operational information into operational capability.

Building upon the principles established in the Sigma Doctrine, this document describes the conceptual operating model that enables operational signals to become trusted context and ultimately actionable operational experiences.

Unlike the Doctrine, which explains why Sigma exists, this document explains how Sigma operates.

---

## Audience

The Sigma Operating Model is intended for leaders responsible for designing, building, and operating Sigma capabilities.

This includes operational leadership, product leadership, architecture leadership, engineering leadership, and delivery teams responsible for implementing Sigma's operating model.

---

## Scope

This document defines:

- Sigma's conceptual operating model.
- The transformation flow between each conceptual layer.
- The responsibilities of each layer.
- The organizational capabilities required to support the model.
- The relationships between Sigma's strategic building blocks.

---

## Out of Scope

This document intentionally excludes:

- Product specifications
- User experience design
- Technical architecture
- Data models
- APIs
- Delivery plans
- Implementation details

These topics are documented within their respective knowledge domains.
